Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly when using the Service, such as your name, email address, and password used to create and access your account. We also collect the financial data you choose to log, including expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may additionally collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help keep the Service secure and reliable.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate and improve the Service — for example, to authenticate your account, display your expense history, and generate summaries and category breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes or sell it to third parties. We may use aggregated, non-identifying information to understand overall usage patterns and improve Spendly's features.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical and administrative safeguards. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing methods and are never kept in plain text. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party services for functions such as hosting, infrastructure, or font delivery. These providers only receive the minimum information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your personal financial data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website. We're happy to address any questions about your data and how Spendly uses it.
